Life outside the lab

Fishing

When I'm not building process models, there's a good chance I'm out by the water with a fishing rod. Fishing is how I slow down and reset: a quiet bank, a line in the water, and the small thrill when something finally bites.

Most of my trips are with my labmate and good friend William Farlessyost. We head out to local lakes and ponds whenever we get the chance, trade tips on bait and casting, and celebrate every catch, however small.

It also connects back to my research. I've worked on stream networks and freshwater biodiversity, and spending time on the water is a good reminder of the ecosystems that work is meant to protect.

Tennis

Tennis is my other favorite way to unwind. I've played a lot of it with my friends Abhimanyu Rajshekhar and William Farlessyost, and I really enjoy it: long rallies, friendly competition and plenty of laughs on court.
